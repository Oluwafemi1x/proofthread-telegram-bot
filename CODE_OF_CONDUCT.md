# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ProofThread a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional community is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
